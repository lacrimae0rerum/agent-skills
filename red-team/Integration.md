# Red Team Integration Guide

**Use BEFORE red-team:**
- `research` (optional skill) - Gather context, find precedents

**Use DURING red-team:**
- `first-principles` (Deconstruct intent) - Decomposition methodology: break arguments into fundamental parts
- `first-principles` (Challenge intent) - Classify constraints as hard/soft/assumption
- Parallel assault: one persona per attacker, as subagents in parallel when the agent has them (an independent model per persona where available), otherwise played in sequence

**Use AFTER red-team:**
- `extract-wisdom` (optional skill) - Highest-signal critiques

The most devastating critiques come from challenging hidden assumptions. Invocation: "Use the first-principles skill's Challenge intent on the stated constraints before parallel analysis"
